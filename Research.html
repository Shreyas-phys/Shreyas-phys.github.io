Research

My research spans quantum computing, quantum metrology, and the dynamics of quantum many-body systems, with the aim of understanding what quantum hardware can do and using it to study emergent phenomena. Papers, preprints, and code are collected below.

Research Interests

Quantum Computing & Error Correction

Fault-tolerant quantum simulation, quantum error correction, decoherence-free subspaces, and resource estimates for near- and long-term hardware.

Quantum Metrology & Sensing

Phase estimation with entangled photon states in interferometers, and adaptive and non-adaptive measurement strategies.

Long-Range Quantum Systems

Information propagation, nonlocal light cones, and programmable signal generation in long-range interacting spin chains.

Non-Equilibrium Many-Body Dynamics

Many-body localization, ergodicity breaking, shielding effects, and quantum chaos in interacting quantum systems.

Quantum State Preparation & Control

Accelerated state preparation, quantum Mpemba effects, and robustness of control protocols to disorder.

Computational Quantum Physics

Exact diagonalization, numerical simulation of quantum dynamics, and machine learning tools for quantum systems.
