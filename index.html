Welcome

Hi, I'm Shreyas, a physicist with a Ph.D. from Tulane University. I'm driven by a simple goal: helping build a quantum computer that is genuinely useful, and finding the problems where it will matter most.

My work sits where quantum hardware, quantum information, and many-body physics meet. On one side, I'm interested in what real quantum devices can do, from fault-tolerant simulation and error correction to precision measurement. On the other, I want to use quantum computers and many-body theory to understand emergent phenomena: how collective behavior, information flow, and new phases of matter arise from simple interacting parts.

When I'm not thinking about quantum systems, I'm usually somewhere outdoors: on a backpacking trail, a long run, in the pool, or on the mat for martial arts. They give me peace of mind and keep me disciplined. They also feed the same curiosity that drives my research. Whether it's a mountain ridge or an open problem, I'm drawn to finding out what's on the other side, and to figuring out how I can make a difference.
